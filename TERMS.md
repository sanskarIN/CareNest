# CareNest Terms of Use

CareNest is provided as an open-source organizational application under the Apache License 2.0. These terms summarize intended product-use boundaries and do not replace the license text or legal obligations that may apply in a user's jurisdiction.

## Organizational, not medical

CareNest does not provide medical diagnosis, dosage determination/inference, treatment recommendations, clinical medication-interaction checking, clinical risk scoring, verified adherence records or emergency services.

Users are responsible for following instructions from qualified professionals, checking actual medicine supplies and using appropriate emergency/clinical services when needed.

## Reminder limitation

Reminder delivery can be affected by operating-system permissions, battery/background restrictions, force-stop/shutdown, device state, time-zone/clock changes, platform policy and other factors outside CareNest control.

Do not rely on CareNest as the sole source of emergency or time-critical medical information.

## Local data responsibility

Users are responsible for protecting their device, optional app lock, exported files, backup passwords and external backup/share destinations.

CareNest does not claim transparent whole-database encryption. Imported document payloads and manual backups use separate encryption protections as documented.

## Local-first boundary

Current v1 does not require a CareNest account/backend and does not automatically upload normal health-organizer records to a CareNest cloud service.

External share/export/calendar/browser actions are explicit user handoffs and can be governed by third-party services after control leaves CareNest.

## External copies

Documents, reports, backups or calendar data explicitly exported/shared can be retained by the receiving application/service/device. Deleting CareNest-owned local data does not automatically remove those external copies.
