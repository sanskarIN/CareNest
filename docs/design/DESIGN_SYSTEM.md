# Design System

CareNest aims for calm, clear, non-clinical organization.

## Tokens

- Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32.
- Corner radii: 10 (controls), 16 (cards), 24 (hero surfaces).
- Minimum touch target: 44×44 logical units.
- Typography: platform default, scalable; title 28, section 20, body 16, caption 13.
- Motion: short, optional, never required to understand state; reduced-motion setting disables decorative transitions.
- Color roles: background, surface, primary, on-primary, text, muted-text, success, warning, danger. Never rely on color alone.

## Brand

Logo combines a gentle nest/shield outline with a small calendar check. It intentionally avoids a red cross and medical-accreditation imagery.

Watermark `Made by the Sanskar` belongs in splash/About/footer areas, never over user health content.
