## Official storefront

**Gumroad:** https://ramsandesh.gumroad.com

This storefront may contain separate digital products such as books, learning resources, project material, templates, documentation bundles, or other independently published content.

## CareNest boundary

The Gumroad storefront is **separate from CareNest health functionality**.

- CareNest does not require a Gumroad purchase.
- Gumroad purchases do not unlock medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, dosage decisions, reminder priority, reminder reliability, emergency assistance, or access to user health data.
- CareNest does not send health records to Gumroad.
- The CareNest application package intentionally does not embed the Gumroad destination or promotional artwork in store-distributed builds.
- Repository and documentation links may promote the storefront while the application runtime remains local-first and store-safe.

## Where the storefront is promoted

The repository may surface the storefront in:

- the main README;
- documentation hubs and catalogs;
- support and contributor documentation;
- GitHub funding/project metadata where appropriate;
- project/marketing documentation;
- repository-only promotional artwork;
- release/documentation handoff records.

The storefront must not be treated as a clinical service or a CareNest feature entitlement.
